Building a desktop setup brick-by-brick teaches you exactly how every daemon and layer interacts.

### Key Highlights

1. **Hyprland** for smooth, modern Wayland tiling.
2. **Fish Shell** for sensible auto-suggestions out of the box.
3. **No Bloat** — only the packages and services explicitly enabled.
